# Code of Conduct

This is a small community around a practical goal: helping Minecraft server owners fix problems. We want everyone to be able to take part, whether they run a hobby server or a large network, and whether this is their first pull request or their hundredth.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Most mistakes are misunderstandings. Ask before you decide that someone meant harm.
- **Criticise the work, not the person.** "This regex also matches the normal start-up line" helps. "This is sloppy" does not.
- **Be patient with newcomers.** Explain what you would change and why. A reviewer's comment should leave the author able to fix it.
- **Respect privacy.** Never post another person's logs, IP addresses, names or messages. Sanitize everything, including screenshots.
- **Stay on topic and keep it safe for work.** No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised or violent content, or unsolicited advertising.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, threats, doxxing, spam, and pushing a contribution by pressuring maintainers. Using a request for beta access to harass someone is also not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people from the repository. Serious or repeated violations also mean the beta access earned through the repository can be revoked.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the **Security** tab (choose "Report a vulnerability") and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are handled in confidence.

Language: write in Russian or English, whichever you are comfortable with.
